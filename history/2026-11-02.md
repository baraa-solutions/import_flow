## 3. Smart RFQ Generator Implementation (BP-008 & Freight Operations)

### A. Context & Architectural Problem
- Designed and implemented the automated multi-modal Smart RFQ Generator service (`modules/freight_quotations/smart_rfq_service.py`), eliminating common human errors identified in quotation requests:
  - Weight integrity violations (Net Weight > Gross Weight).
  - Missing volumetric ratios and chargeable weight comparisons.
  - Absence of preferential trade agreements (EUR.1, UK Origin, EUR-MED).
  - Port-specific terminal restrictions (Direct Dekheila Terminal, strictly avoiding TMT Yard).
  - Container-specific weight and stacking limits (20' GP heavy road weight > 21,000 kg and double-stackable pallets > 11 pallets).

### B. Implementation Details
- **Divisor & Mode Distinction:**
  - `AIR Freight`: Uses IATA Divisor **6,000 cm³/kg** ($1\text{ CBM} = 166.67\text{ kg}$).
  - `COURIER (Express)`: Uses Express Courier Divisor **5,000 cm³/kg** ($1\text{ CBM} = 200.00\text{ kg}$), computing a 20% higher volumetric weight for express parcel shipments.
  - `SEA LCL`: Computes actual volume CBM and handles `is_stackable=False` by flagging dead-freight footprint and full container height clearance.
  - `SEA FCL`: Detects heavy road limits on 20' containers and flags double-stackable configurations.
- **REST Endpoints & Schemas:**
  - Added `SmartRFQPackageItem`, `SmartRFQGenerateRequest`, `SmartRFQMetrics`, `SmartRFQResponse` in `modules/freight_quotations/schemas.py`.
  - Added endpoints in `modules/freight_quotations/router.py`:
    - `GET /api/v1/freight-quotations/smart-rfq/{import_file_id}/preview` (Permission: `quotations.view`).
    - `POST /api/v1/freight-quotations/smart-rfq/generate` (Permission: `quotations.manage`).
