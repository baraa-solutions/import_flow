## 3. Smart RFQ Generator Implementation (BP-008 & Freight Operations)

### A. Context & Architectural Problem
- Designed and implemented the automated multi-modal Smart RFQ Generator service (`modules/freight_quotations/smart_rfq_service.py`), eliminating common human errors identified in quotation requests:
  - Weight integrity violations (Net Weight > Gross Weight).
  - Missing volumetric ratios and chargeable weight comparisons.
  - Absence of preferential trade agreements (EUR.1, UK Origin, EUR-MED).
  - Port-specific terminal restrictions (Direct Dekheila Terminal, strictly avoiding TMT Yard).
  - Container-specific weight and stacking limits (20' GP heavy road weight > 21,000 kg and double-stackable pallets > 11 pallets).

### B. Implementation Details
- **Divisor & Mode Distinction:**
  - `AIR Freight`: Uses IATA Divisor **6,000 cm³/kg** ($1\text{ CBM} = 166.67\text{ kg}$).
  - `COURIER (Express)`: Uses Express Courier Divisor **5,000 cm³/kg** ($1\text{ CBM} = 200.00\text{ kg}$), computing a 20% higher volumetric weight for express parcel shipments.
  - `SEA LCL`: Computes actual volume CBM and handles `is_stackable=False` by flagging dead-freight footprint and full container height clearance.
  - `SEA FCL`: Detects heavy road limits on 20' containers and flags double-stackable configurations.
- **REST Endpoints & Schemas:**
  - Added `SmartRFQPackageItem`, `SmartRFQGenerateRequest`, `SmartRFQMetrics`, `SmartRFQResponse` in `modules/freight_quotations/schemas.py`.
  - Added endpoints in `modules/freight_quotations/router.py`:
    - `GET /api/v1/freight-quotations/smart-rfq/{import_file_id}/preview` (Permission: `quotations.view`).
    - `POST /api/v1/freight-quotations/smart-rfq/generate` (Permission: `quotations.manage`).

## Air Freight & Express Courier Domain Rules & Dual Volumetric Divisor Implementation

### 1. Architectural & Domain Logic Overview
Implemented comprehensive domain logic and backend rules for Air Freight vs. Express Courier cargo, dual volumetric weight calculation, Egyptian Customs Law 207/2020 postal parcel exemption rules, automatic lifecycle stage skipping, and mandatory stage gating:
- **Dual Volumetric Divisor:**
  - IATA Standard Air Freight: Divisor 6000.0 ($1\text{ CBM} = 166.67\text{ kg}$).
  - Express Courier (DHL, FedEx, UPS, Aramex): Divisor 5000.0 ($1\text{ CBM} = 200.00\text{ kg}$).
  - Mathematical evaluation: $\text{Volumetric Weight} = \frac{\text{Length} \times \text{Width} \times \text{Height}}{\text{Divisor}} \times \text{Quantity}$.
  - Chargeable Weight: $\max(\text{Gross Weight}, \text{Volumetric Weight})$.
  - Comparative analytics via `calculate_air_vs_courier_comparison(...)` determining chargeable weight divergence and dollar cost impact.
- **Egyptian Customs Law 207/2020 Postal Parcel Exemption Thresholds:**
  - Air & Courier Shipments:
    - If $\text{Gross Weight} > 50.0\text{ kg}$ OR $\text{Customs Value} > \$2000.0\text{ USD}$:
      - `acid_requirement = "MANDATORY"`
      - `acid_exemption_reason = "Exceeds courier threshold: weight > 50kg or value > $2000"`
    - If $\text{Gross Weight} \le 50.0\text{ kg}$ AND $\text{Customs Value} \le \$2000.0\text{ USD}$:
      - `acid_requirement = "EXEMPT_POSTAL"`
      - `acid_exemption_reason = "Postal parcel under 50kg and $2000 (Article 1, Customs Law 207/2020)"`
  - Ocean Shipments:
    - Always `acid_requirement = "MANDATORY"`.
- **Automatic Lifecycle Stage Skipping for Postal Exempt Shipments:**
  - `EXEMPT_POSTAL` shipments automatically include `"STEP_05"` (ACID Operations), `"STEP_10"` (CargoX Upload), and `"STEP_12"` (Bank Form 4) in `skipped_stages`.
  - These stages are bypassed cleanly without triggering skip policy restrictions.
  - Audit log activity is logged for the postal exemption under Customs Law 207/2020.
- **Strict Stage Gate for Mandatory Shipments:**
  - Advancing past `STEP_05` without a verified 19-digit numeric `acid_number` raises `HTTPException(status_code=400)` blocking transition.
  - Shipments with `EXEMPT_POSTAL` bypass this gate cleanly.
