## 1. Overview & Objective
Executed **Track 3: Unsaved Changes Guard / Data Loss Prevention** across ImportFlow ERP. Standardized dirty-form tracking, draft auto-recovery, and route/dialog pop interception to prevent data loss when operators navigate away, hit `Esc`, close workspace tabs, or click "Back to Dashboard".

## 2. Infrastructure & Key Additions
1. **`FormDraftService` (`frontend/lib/core/services/form_draft_service.dart`)**:
   - Centralized local draft caching service backed by `SharedPreferences`.
   - Methods: `saveDraft`, `loadDraft`, `clearDraft`, `hasDraft`.
   - Standardized draft key prefixes (`form_draft_`) with auto-timestamping (`lastSaved`).
   - Refactored `po_draft_manager.dart` to delegate to `FormDraftService`.
2. **`UnsavedChangesGuard` & `UnsavedChangesDialog` (`frontend/lib/core/widgets/`)**:
   - Standardized `PopScope` wrapper that intercepts route popping when `isDirty == true`.
   - Automatically synchronizes tab dirty state with `workspaceTabsProvider.activeTab.isDirty`.
   - Presents operator options: Discard (onDiscard), Stay, Save Draft & Exit (onSave).
3. **Scaffold Integration (`VerticalStageScaffold` & `DedicatedStageScaffold`)**:
   - Upgraded both foundational scaffolds to accept `isDirty`, `onSave`, and `onDiscard`.
   - Scaffolds automatically wrap body content with `UnsavedChangesGuard(isDirty: isDirty, ...)`.
4. **`BackToDashboardButton` Interception**:
   - Checked `ref.read(workspaceTabsProvider).activeTab.isDirty`.
   - Prompts `UnsavedChangesDialog.show` before returning to dashboard if dirty changes exist.
