### 📊 Technical Changes
- `scenario_item_totals`: Multiplies 40ft by `container_40ft_qty`, 20ft by `container_20ft_qty`, LCL by `lcl_cbm_qty`, per-container surcharges (SOLAS VGM, VGM notification) by `(container_40ft_qty + container_20ft_qty)`, and flat charges by 1.
- Converts each component into `quotation_currency` with commercial rates.
- Raises 409 `SS_FX_RATE_MISSING` if a rate is missing.
- Defensive legacy fallback for pre-existing DB rows without breakdown flags.
- Downstream services compare pure ocean/international freight rather than quote totals containing clearance/port expenses.
