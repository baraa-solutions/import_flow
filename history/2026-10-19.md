### 📊 Technical Functionality
- **Allocation Rules:**
  1. No allocation rows for PO: takes full lines (100% share).
  2. PO has allocations across any file, but none for current file: skips PO completely and emits `SETTLEMENT_PO_NOT_ALLOCATED`.

  4. PO-level allocations (`po_item_id is None`): calculates `shipped_amount_fob / total_amount_fob` ratio (or quantity ratio fallback when FOB is 0) and allocates pro-rata to remaining unallocated lines.
