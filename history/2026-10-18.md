## BEX-T15: Audit Trail Actor, Audit-Log Access, Report Exports

### 🎯 Objective
Resolve MAJOR findings `BEX-F16` and `BEX-F33` from `docs/plans/backend-review/be-cross-review.md`:
1. Make `performed_by: str` mandatory in `AuditLogService.log_activity` (removed default `"System Admin"`), enforcing explicit actor provenance across all modules.
2. Wire actual actor (`actor.username if actor else "SYSTEM"`) into `modules/auth/service.py` (`register_user`, `update_user`, `toggle_user_status`, `update_user_permissions`).
3. Wire explicit `performed_by="SYSTEM"` in remaining master-data audit calls (`modules/external_service_providers/repository.py`, `modules/import_companies/service.py`, `modules/suppliers/service.py`).
4. Secure `/api/v1/audit-logs` routes by replacing plain `get_current_user` with `require_permission("audit_logs.view")`.
5. Move export row formatting from `modules/import_files/router.py` to `modules/import_files/service.py` via `build_export_rows(files, format="excel"|"pdf")`.
6. Implement `None`-safe formatting (`f"{(f.progress_percent or 0):.0f}%"`) so legacy rows with `progress_percent=NULL` do not cause 500 errors.
7. Require `reports.export` permission on `GET /api/v1/import-files/export-excel` and `GET /api/v1/import-files/export-pdf`.
8. Add comprehensive unit test suite `tests/unit/test_audit_trail_actor.py`.
