A full quality review of every import-cycle stage, from planning to file closure, with a clearer new design for most screens.
Purchase orders: a new order opens empty, number fields accept decimals without closing after each digit, weights keep full precision, and editing an order no longer deletes lines linked to shipments.
Smart invoice extraction reads the right currency from the invoice total, the HS code as printed and weights with decimals.
Permissions on every screen: approving, paying, releasing and closing need the right permission, and the approver is recorded from the signed-in account.
No fixed exchange rates or percentages inside the app: rates come from the customs tariff, currency tables and settings, and unknown data stays empty instead of made-up defaults.
Financial approval: a request cannot be paid before it is approved, and SWIFT matching checks the currency and reference.
Documents: the draft B/L review needs approval by two different people, and Form 46 computes VAT on the correct base with schedule tax and import fee.
Customs clearance: one duty-total formula, totals locked after assessment, and no release before full payment or while under quarantine.
Demurrage: agreed free days apply on every recalculation, and pushed charges update the settlement automatically.
Warehouse and closure: goods receipts reject unbalanced quantities, and the final close checks its conditions on the server.
Financial settlement: approval keeps the stored invoices, lab fees are not counted twice, and the journal entry always balances.
Lists no longer fail because of old records, and administrator access can be restored on older installs.
